Polina is a PhD student on the [UCL Birkbeck MRC doctoral training program](https://www.uclbbk-mrcdtp.ac.uk/), co-supervised by [Julia Harris](https://www.sainsburywellcome.org/people/julia-harris). Polina has a BSc in Psychology and an MSc in Cognitive Neuroscience. She is interested in utilising human and rodent electrophysiology to better understand the mechanistic contribution of sleep to learning and memory.
